# 📊 Business Insights 360 – Power BI Dashboard

> **From Data to Decisions**

An end-to-end **Business Intelligence and Data Analytics project** built using Microsoft Power BI to provide a 360° view of business performance across Finance, Sales, Marketing, Supply Chain and Executive functions.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

**Business Insights 360** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to help business stakeholders understand performance from multiple perspectives.

The dashboard brings together key business metrics and enables users to analyse:

- Revenue and profitability
- Customer and product performance
- Regional performance
- Marketing performance
- Forecast accuracy
- Supply chain risks
- Business trends and KPIs

The objective is to transform business data into **actionable insights that support data-driven decision-making**.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

As the business expands across different markets and channels, decision-makers need a consolidated view of business performance.

The challenge is to move from:

**Raw Data → Reports → Analysis → Insights → Decisions**

The Business Insights 360 dashboard addresses this by bringing multiple business functions into a single interactive reporting solution.

---

## 📌 Dashboard Views

The project contains five major analytical views:

### 💰 1. Finance View

Provides visibility into:

- Profit & Loss
- Gross Sales
- Net Sales
- COGS
- Gross Margin
- Gross Margin %
- Operational Expenses
- Net Profit
- Net Profit %

The Finance view helps identify the relationship between revenue, costs and profitability.

---

### 🛒 2. Sales View

Focuses on:

- Customer performance
- Product performance
- Net Sales
- Gross Margin
- Gross Margin %
- Customer contribution
- Sales trends
- Unit economics

Users can analyse top and bottom customers and products and compare their performance.

---

### 📣 3. Marketing View

Provides analysis of:

- Revenue
- Gross Margin
- Net Profit
- Regional performance
- Marketing-related business metrics
- Segment performance
- Trends

This view helps understand how different markets and segments contribute to overall business performance.

---

### 🚚 4. Supply Chain View

Focuses on forecasting and supply chain performance.

Key metrics include:

- Forecast Accuracy %
- Net Error
- Absolute Error
- Customer Forecast Accuracy
- Product Forecast Accuracy
- Risk classification

Customers and products can be analysed based on forecast accuracy and risk indicators.

---

### 👔 5. Executive View

Provides a consolidated management-level overview of the business.

Key KPIs include:

- Net Sales
- Gross Margin %
- Net Profit %
- Forecast Accuracy %
- Revenue contribution
- Top customers
- Top products
- Regional performance
- Channel contribution
- Business trends

The Executive View allows stakeholders to quickly understand overall business performance and identify areas requiring attention.

---

## 📊 Key Business Insights

The dashboard enables analysis of several important business questions:

### Revenue & Profitability

- How is Net Sales performing?
- How is Gross Margin changing?
- Which regions contribute the most revenue?
- Which regions are profitable or loss-making?
- How are costs impacting profitability?

### Customer Performance

- Which customers contribute the highest Net Sales?
- Which customers generate higher Gross Margin?
- Which customers show declining margin performance?

### Product Performance

- Which products are driving revenue?
- Which products contribute significantly to gross margin?
- Which products require further attention?

### Supply Chain

- How accurate are the forecasts?
- Which customers have forecast accuracy issues?
- Which products show excess inventory or out-of-stock risk?
- How is forecast accuracy changing over time?

### Executive Performance

- What are the key business KPIs?
- How is revenue contribution changing?
- Which regions, customers and products are driving performance?
- Where should management focus its attention?
